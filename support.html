Contact VIARUM Support

Questions about the app, accounts, subscriptions, routes, rallies or a problem you've encountered? Email us at:

viarumsupport@gmail.com

Describe what happened and, if useful, include your iPhone model, iOS version, VIARUM app version and a screenshot. Please don't send passwords, card numbers or other sensitive credentials.

Help topics

Signing in or accessing your account

If you can't sign in or your saved data seems to be missing, contact support with the sign-in method you used. Never send your password.

VIARUM+ subscriptions

For subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store, manage or cancel them in iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. If an existing purchase isn't recognized, try Restore Purchases inside VIARUM. For App Store refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Routes, navigation, rallies and events

Report an incorrect route, navigation issue or event listing by emailing the item's name, what happened and any supporting screenshot. Always follow posted road signs, road conditions and traffic laws.

Hotels and restaurant reservations

VIARUM may help you discover real places and open third-party booking or reservation websites. Any confirmed reservation, availability or payment is handled by the relevant provider. Contact that provider for changes to a booking made through its service.

Privacy, personal data and account deletion

Privacy controls and account deletion options are available in the VIARUM app. If you need help accessing or deleting your information, email support. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Safety and community reports

Use the reporting options available inside VIARUM or email details of an unsafe or inappropriate post, profile or event. VIARUM isn't an emergency service. Contact local emergency services in an emergency.
