About this policy

VIARUM is a worldwide platform for driving, road trips, rallies and automotive communities. This policy explains how information is handled in the app, including VIARUM+ and VIARUM All Access, on our public website, and when you contact us. The information involved depends on the features you use, the content you provide and the permissions you allow.

For privacy questions or requests, contact viarumsupport@gmail.com. VIARUM is responsible for explaining its use of your information and the service providers used to deliver its features.

Account information and public content

When you sign in, VIARUM receives an account identifier and the profile information supplied through your chosen sign-in method, which can include your name, email address and profile image. We also store the profile fields, preferences, saved items, vehicles, trips and participation records you create or choose to save online.

Your driver profile, public garage information, posts, comments, follows, shared routes and participation in public features can be visible to other people. Private club, conversation and rally features restrict access according to their membership and roles. Information supplied to an organizer or another participant is available to those people as part of the feature.

Ordinary profile, vehicle, event and community photos can be accessed through public media links. Club photos use separate access controls. Review content before sharing it, and avoid including private documents, addresses or other information you do not want the intended audience to see. Other people may keep their own copies of content you share.

Location, navigation and live sharing

With your permission, VIARUM uses device location for maps, nearby places, planning and navigation. A navigation session you start can continue using location while your phone is locked or the app is in the background. You can end navigation and change VIARUM's location permission in iOS Settings.

Live sharing with rally or drive participants is a separate choice. You select the sharing feature and audience, can stop sharing, and sessions expire. Current sharing sessions are limited to four hours; reconnecting can resume an unexpired session. Ending a sharing session does not end all other uses of location.

Saved trips, routes, meeting points, road reports, road-completion records and help requests can contain location information. Resolving a help request or removing an expired item from the active map does not necessarily delete its stored record. A help-request location is supplied separately from live-sharing consent. AI requests can also include location, as described below.

AI assistance and your choices

The app is configured to send VIARUM AI requests through Rork's AI service for processing with Google Gemini. Depending on the feature, a request can contain your message, recent conversation, the screen or record you are viewing, saved preferences, vehicle details, trip or rally context, dietary requirements, booking or budget context and location. The general assistant can include available precise device location even when your question is not specifically about location. Where rally operations are visible to you, AI context can also include other participants' names, check-in status, captain roles and help-request details. Diagnostic-code explanations use the selected trouble codes and request, rather than automatically attaching your full diagnostic report.

Before ordinary AI sharing begins, the app asks you to approve the disclosed provider and purpose. It remembers that choice for your account on this device and checks it before sending subsequent requests. You can withdraw that approval using Stop Sharing with AI in Privacy & Security on this device. Use that control separately on another device where you have approved sharing. A later request can ask you to approve sharing again. Withdrawal does not recall information already sent or delete copies held by providers.

Dietary and accessibility preferences can reveal sensitive information, including allergies or requirements associated with health, disability or religion. Dietary requirements can be included in AI planning requests. Dietary and accessibility preferences can also be used in relevant place searches and saved with your trip. Turning off AI Memory or personalization does not remove explicit planning inputs or ordinary feature context from an approved request.

Virtual Workshop photos, clips and speech

Virtual Workshop lets you take a photo or record a short clip. The app prepares still images from the clip and, where supported and permitted, transcribes speech on your device. You can review and remove the images or transcript before approving an AI request. Workshop images require a separate approval for each request.

The Workshop AI request sends the reviewed still images, any remaining transcript and vehicle/request context. It does not send the original video or audio. The app clears the selected evidence after a successful response or when you leave or reset the Workshop view; evidence may remain available for retry after a failed request. You can choose to save the transcript and resulting suggestions in a local Workshop case. Clearing local evidence does not erase information already processed by an AI provider.

Glove Box and organizer documents

When you scan or import a document into Glove Box, VIARUM stores the selected original and filing information on the device. Text recognition and suggested categories or record details are generated on the device. A scan or import may be saved for review before recognition finishes. Organizer Documents stores selected scans or imported images/PDFs with the titles, event names and categories you enter; its filing process is separate from Glove Box's automatic text recognition.

These document-storage features do not automatically upload original documents or extracted text to VIARUM's servers or an external AI service. If you share a document or export using the device's sharing tools, the selected copy goes to the recipient or service you choose.

Some local app data may be included in device backups according to your device and backup settings. Organizer document files are configured to be excluded from backup. Do not rely on VIARUM as your only copy of important documents.

All Access records and saved trips

Some automotive workspaces store records locally for the current app account, including diagnostic snapshots, performance results, build plans, ownership records, Workshop cases and the manual organizer ledger. Performance Lab uses location during an active foreground session and can save results and time/speed samples. Its saved performance records do not contain a trail of GPS coordinates.

Saved trips and other account features can also be stored online. Trip records can include origin, destination and stop coordinates, notes, dietary preferences, booking references and cost estimates. The All Access name does not mean every feature is local-only. Public trip-sharing snapshots omit specified private trip fields, but the route and stops you choose to share remain visible to the recipients.

Local record limits can remove older entries: most workspace categories keep up to 250 records, ownership history up to 10,000, and diagnostic history up to 100 reports. These are record-count limits, not guaranteed retention periods or proof that every associated original file has been erased.

AI Memory

AI Memory is separate from the conversation. The app adds a memory when you choose to save it or enter it yourself; it does not automatically save your entire conversation as memory. Saved memories can synchronize with your account online.

You can review, add or delete memories in VIARUM AI Memory. To correct a memory, delete it and add the replacement. You can turn memory and personalization off. This stops saved memories being used for personalization, but does not delete existing memories, explicit planning inputs, other permitted feature context or previous provider copies. Avoid storing unnecessary sensitive information in a memory.

Messages and Rally Radio

Direct messages and rally or club messages are processed to deliver conversations to their permitted audiences. In direct messaging, leaving a conversation removes it from your own conversation list; it does not delete the stored messages or another participant's copy. Message deletion and account deletion are separate controls.

When you use Rally Radio, microphone audio is transmitted through the configured LiveKit service to authorized channel participants. The microphone opens for an authorized transmission and closes when you release the talk control. Account identity, display name, channel and role information support access to the correct room. Radio audio handling is separate from the on-device transcription used in Workshop.

Subscriptions and rally payments

Apple and RevenueCat support VIARUM+ and All Access purchases, restoration and subscription access. VIARUM and RevenueCat process account identifiers, purchase history and entitlement information to provide access, validate purchases and understand subscription activity.

Stripe supports paid rally entry, refunds and organizer onboarding. Payment and identity details are entered on Stripe-hosted pages. VIARUM's reviewed payment flows use transaction references, amounts, currency, status and organizer account information; they do not include a custom card-entry form or store full card numbers in the app. Stripe's handling of information submitted to its pages is additional to VIARUM's own records.

Deleting your VIARUM account or signing out does not itself cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions through Apple's subscription controls.

Notifications, support and technical information

If you allow notifications, we associate an Apple push token and related app/device information with your account to deliver alerts. This can include platform, app version, locale and last registration time. The app attempts to unregister the token on sign-out; tokens reported invalid by Apple are disabled. Notification settings control delivery and do not themselves erase every token record.

If you contact support or report a problem, we receive the information you send. A prepared problem report can include app/build and iOS version information. Service providers also process technical information needed to handle connections and operate their services. We use account, transaction, moderation and technical records to provide support, prevent misuse, protect access and maintain the service.

Service providers and international processing

The services used by a feature can include:

  • Rork for sign-in, app backend and AI gateway services; the app requests Google Gemini for AI responses.
  • Supabase for account-related database records and uploaded media; the backend also includes Cloudflare Worker/Durable Object components for live features and stored rally chat.
  • Apple for sign-in where chosen, purchases, notifications, maps, location-related system services and weather. Google also supports the Google sign-in option.
  • RevenueCat for subscription administration and reporting; Stripe for hosted rally payments and organizer onboarding.
  • LiveKit for Rally Radio; Open-Meteo for a weather fallback; and LiteAPI/Nuitee, when configured, for hotel-property lookup and hosted booking links.
  • GitHub Pages for the public website and email providers when you contact support.

Depending on the feature and provider configuration, information may be processed in countries other than your own. This policy does not promise a particular storage country, immediate provider deletion or exclusion of AI inputs from model training. Provider retention, processing terms and settings must be considered separately from what the app stores or clears on your device. Contact us for questions about a particular request or service.

Retention, deletion and copies

Saved account information supports the features you use, while different local stores have the limits described above. Ending live sharing, closing a help request, leaving a conversation, hiding content and signing out are different actions from deleting the underlying information. Sign-out can retain account-specific local records so they are available when you sign back in.

You can initiate account deletion in Settings. The app reports an error when server cleanup cannot be confirmed. After the server reports success, it clears supported local account records and attempts to remove related files from that device. Removing content from a feature can occur before every original, cached or provider-held copy is removed.

Some transaction, fraud-prevention, security, dispute or legally required records may need to be retained for those specific purposes. The chat-erasure system also retains account-identifier markers to prevent erased messages being recreated. Deletion does not itself remove copies you exported, independent copies saved by other people, or all backups and provider records. For an access or erasure request covering information beyond the available app controls, contact viarumsupport@gmail.com.

Your controls and exports

You can control device permissions in iOS Settings, stop navigation or live sharing, manage notification preferences, withdraw ordinary AI-sharing approval, and review or delete saved memories. Feature-specific controls let you remove records where available.

Privacy & Security offers a local All Access export and a separate local erase control. The export contains supported workspace records and diagnostic reports; it is not a full archive of original Glove Box files, extracted-text files or Organizer Documents and its separate index. Save or share needed original documents separately. Local erasure does not delete your cloud Garage or other cloud account records.

The account-data export downloads the online account records supported by that export. It does not include every original upload, all separate chat storage, every payment/subscription record or all information held by providers. Contact us if you need access to information not included in an export.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent or complain to a privacy regulator. We may need to verify your identity and explain any applicable legal restriction when responding.

Security

VIARUM's online service connections use HTTPS/TLS. The app and backend use account checks and access rules for private features, and local document files use iOS file-protection controls. Public content and public media links are intended to be accessible to their audiences. These controls do not make public uploads private and are not a promise of end-to-end encryption or absolute security.

Website, external links and children

GitHub Pages logs website visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. When you follow a booking, merchant or social-media link, the destination handles the information you provide under its own terms. Those independent services may use cookies or other technology that VIARUM does not control.

VIARUM is intended for people who meet the eligibility requirements in its Terms of Service, and is not directed to children. If you believe a child's information has been provided improperly, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

Policy updates and contact

We will update this policy to reflect changes in features or data handling, and provide additional notice or seek consent when required. For privacy questions, access requests or deletion concerns, contact viarumsupport@gmail.com.
