The promise

Your information is used to provide the features you choose. VIARUM does not sell your personal data, and does not build or sell a personal location profile. There is no continuous public tracking in VIARUM: your position is only shared when you start a sharing session yourself, and only for as long as that session lasts.

Public data

Your username, driver profile, the garage vehicles you choose to make public, your posts, and the routes you share. This is visible to other drivers by design. It is kept until you delete the individual item or your account.

Private account data

Your email address, region, preferences, saved items, and the events you have joined. Used to run your account and your personal features. Kept while your account is active and never exposed through public APIs.

Temporary location data

Your live position is processed only while a rally or drive sharing session you started is active, so other participants and the organizer can coordinate. Sessions expire automatically and you can end one at any time. VIARUM does not keep a permanent public history of where you have been. Coarse or one-off location is also used when you ask for something nearby — a route, a meet, fuel, or mechanical help.

AI memory

Only the preferences you explicitly save — such as fuel choice, hotel style, route preference, or budget. Nothing is written to AI memory automatically, and sensitive data is never stored there. Memory is separate from chat history, and each entry can be viewed, edited, or deleted in Settings › VIARUM AI Memory. You can switch memory and personalization off entirely.

Notification device token

If you turn on notifications, VIARUM stores the Apple-issued push token for that device so Apple can deliver alerts to it. The token identifies a device, not a location. It is removed when you sign out on that device, when Apple retires it, or when you delete your account.

Photos and camera

Photos you pick or capture are uploaded only for the vehicle, post, or profile you attach them to. They are deleted when you delete that item, and all at once when you delete your account. VIARUM does not scan your photo library in the background.

Security data

Login events, device records, and session records, used to detect suspicious access and protect your account. These are rotated on a security schedule. Accounts with elevated permissions are held to stronger security requirements.

Service providers

VIARUM relies on a small number of processors to function: Apple (in-app purchases and push delivery), a subscription management provider that reports your entitlement status, a hosted database and storage provider for your account data and uploads, an AI provider that processes the prompts you send, and — only where you pay for a rally through VIARUM — a payment processor. Card details go directly to the payment processor. VIARUM never sees or stores your card number.

How your data is protected

Traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS and data is encrypted at rest. Access is enforced with row-level security and server-side authorization, plus rate limiting, input validation, signed storage URLs, audit logging, and separated development, staging, and production environments. Secret API keys are never embedded in the app.

Children

VIARUM is not intended for children. You must be old enough to hold a valid driving licence in your country, or at minimum 16, to create an account. If we learn an account belongs to a child, it is removed.

Your rights

You can export your account data as a file from Settings, delete individual items at any time, and permanently delete your account in-app. Depending on where you live you may also have rights to access, correct, restrict, or object to processing. Write to viarumsupport@gmail.com and VIARUM will action the request.
